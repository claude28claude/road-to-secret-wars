III

The Domes of Battleworld

One planet. Forty-one domains, each cut from a universe that died. Twenty-two that matter most, in detail.

22 shown

Battleworld is not a place Doom found — it is a place he built. When the multiverse collapses, Doom salvages the pieces he wants and welds them into a single world, then writes himself in as its god. Every dome is a slice of a dead reality with its own laws, its own ruler, and no memory that anything else ever existed. Crossing between them without Doom’s leave is a capital crime. Nothing below is confirmed MCU plot — the domes are drawn from the 2015 Secret Wars comic, and the MCU column is the case for each one, graded honestly.

The rest of the map

The 2015 comic mapped 41 domains in total. These nineteen are the deep cuts — smaller, stranger, and mostly there to prove the planet is bigger than any one story. Marvel could pull any of them out of the drawer for a single shot.
